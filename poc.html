HOW A CALL RUNS
1. Queue

Contact list loaded, schedule and retry policy applied

2. Gate

DND, consent and calling-window checks. Any failure stops the dial

3. Dial

Outbound call placed through the telephony provider

4. Converse

Agent follows the configured script, adapts to the reply

5. Escalate

Uncertain or sensitive turns hand off to a person

6. Record

Typed outcome written back. Transcript retention is opt-in
